that left her fearing for her life. This experience has left its scars, with her mind often
oscillating between the depths of paralyzing dread and numbing disconnection. Although she appears
to navigate the world with ease, Sarah is constantly battling the invisible triggers that weave

a perpetual state of threat, often leading her to overanalyze her surroundings. This heightened

others fully. She often second-guesses her thoughts and feelings—a continuous mental cycle that
leaves her exhausted.

To cope with her distress, Sarah has adopted a series of maladaptive strategies. While she strives
to appear composed, she finds herself slipping into isolation, avoiding situations where she might
encounter her triggers. Though she recognizes this avoidance is detrimental, the fear it breeds is
too overwhelming to confront. Her life has become a balancing act—between the desire to regain her
previous self and the haunting specter of her trauma that looms large.

Sarah occasionally seeks solace in art, channeling her pain into her designs, creating pieces that
resonate with struggles and resilience. But as much as she attempts to express herself through her
creativity, the negative appraisals of her trauma often seep into her work. Doubts about her self-
worth and questions surrounding her abilities haunt her, making it challenging to fully embrace her
artistic gifts. This internal conflict shapes her interactions, leaving her feeling deeply
misunderstood even when surrounded by others.

In her quest for healing, Sarah is slowly learning to face her fears and challenge her negative
appraisals, though the path is fraught with difficulty. She seeks therapy to address her PTSD,
hoping to transform her relationship with her trauma and, ultimately, reclaim her life from the
grips of fear. A journey of self-discovery and empowerment awaits her, as she strives to move beyond
the shadows and rediscover the vibrant colors she once brought into the world.
